# War Consequences
### Every war has consequences. Every nation can fall.

**War Consequences** is a Hearts of Iron IV mod that transforms warfare into a complex simulation of political, military, economic and social instability.

In vanilla Hearts of Iron IV, wars are primarily fought over territory, resources and military objectives. But what happens behind the front lines? What happens when a country loses too many soldiers, when its economy collapses, when its population loses faith in the government, or when its army no longer supports its leaders?

War Consequences explores these questions by introducing a dynamic system of national crises that can reshape the political landscape of the entire world.

## 🌍 Features

### ⚔️ Military Consequences
War can devastate even the strongest nations.

- Military breakdown and loss of operational effectiveness.
- Desertions, mutinies and declining military organization.
- General mobilization in desperate situations.
- Loss of strategic territory and military infrastructure.
- Increasing pressure on governments following major defeats.

### 🏛️ Political Instability
A government cannot remain in power forever when its country is falling apart.

- Military coups led by generals and military leadership.
- Political coups and government overthrows.
- Revolutions capable of bringing entirely new regimes to power.
- Communist, socialist, fascist, democratic and nationalist uprisings.
- Government reshuffles and national unity governments.
- Political radicalization and growing opposition movements.

### 🔥 Civil Wars and Revolutions
A war abroad can ignite a war at home.

- Civil wars triggered by severe national instability.
- Revolutionary movements challenging existing governments.
- Armed factions fighting for control of the country.
- Political and territorial fragmentation.
- Foreign involvement and support for opposing factions when possible.

### 💰 Economic Collapse
War does not only destroy armies. It can destroy entire economies.

- Industrial shutdowns and production losses.
- Resource shortages.
- Infrastructure disruption.
- Emergency requisitions.
- Economic crises caused by prolonged warfare.
- Post-war reconstruction and industrial recovery.

### 🗺️ Territorial Consequences
The territorial integrity of a nation is never guaranteed.

- Separatist movements and independence struggles.
- Loss of control over important regions.
- Government collapse following territorial defeats.
- Governments in exile.
- Attempts to restore national unity after the conflict.

### 📰 World News System
The world does not stop turning just because you are fighting your own war.

War Consequences introduces a global news system inspired by Hearts of Iron IV's newspaper and world news events.

Players can receive reports about major developments in other countries, including:

- Military coups.
- Revolutions and civil wars.
- Government collapses.
- Separatist declarations.
- Separate peace negotiations.
- General mobilizations.
- Major economic and administrative crises.

Every major crisis can become an international event, allowing players to follow the changing political landscape of the world.

### 📋 National Decisions
New decision categories allow governments to respond to their own crises.

- Declare states of emergency.
- Strengthen government authority.
- Organize national mobilization.
- Negotiate with political opposition.
- Suppress demonstrations and uprisings.
- Prevent or respond to military coups.
- Support or resist revolutionary movements.
- Rebuild national industry and restore stability.

### ♻️ Recovery and Reconstruction
A country can fall apart, but it can also recover.

- Restore industrial production.
- Rebuild military forces.
- Recover political stability.
- Re-establish administrative control.
- Reintegrate territories where possible.
- Restore national legitimacy after a crisis.

## ⚙️ Dynamic Crisis System

War Consequences monitors several aspects of national stability:

| Crisis indicator | What it represents |
|---|---|
| Military Crisis | Army losses and military deterioration |
| Economic Crisis | Industrial and economic disruption |
| Political Crisis | Government legitimacy and political instability |
| Social Crisis | Popular unrest and internal tensions |
| Territorial Crisis | Occupation and territorial fragmentation |
| Administrative Crisis | Government and state functionality |

These indicators interact with the country's situation and help determine which consequences may occur.

Events are not simply scripted to happen at fixed dates. Their probability depends on the evolution of the war and the country's internal condition.

## 🎯 Our Philosophy

**No country is completely safe from the consequences of war.**

A military superpower can experience a revolution. A democratic government can be overthrown by its own generals. A victorious nation can suffer an economic crisis. A country on the brink of defeat can recover against all expectations.

War Consequences aims to make every conflict unpredictable, consequential and immersive.

The battlefield is only one part of the war. The real consequences can reach every corner of the world.
